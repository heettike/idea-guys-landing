// the experiment

this is not entertainment.
this is research.

lab rats is a controlled experiment in human psychology, speculation, ambition, and power.

what happens when you put 10 builders in a pressure cooker — give them 14 days, limited resources, and a singular mission: generate $100,000 in real revenue or be eliminated?

we're not building a reality show. we're running a live simulation of founder psychology under extreme conditions. the $lab token isn't just speculation — it's a mechanism for the audience to directly influence outcomes through economic action. burn enough tokens, eliminate a competitor. the crowd becomes the invisible hand.

this is education disguised as chaos. every builder's journey — their wins, their breakdowns, their pivots at 3am — becomes a case study for the next generation of solo founders. we're documenting what it actually takes to build a one-person business from zero to revenue in public.

the goal isn't to crown a winner. it's to expose the truth about building. the pain, the luck, the skill, the politics of attention. all of it, unfiltered.
