The Thesis

Speculation Meets Creation

A 1-person, $1 billion company isn't far-fetched anymore. With AI tools, permissionless distribution, and global markets — the leverage available to a single founder has never been higher.

We want to prove it in the shortest time possible.

The Mission

10 Builders.
One Goal.

Idea Guys is where speculation meets creation. 10 builders enter. A single mission: generate $100,000 in real revenue using AI or be eliminated.

First to hit the target wins $100k on top. No teams. No funding rounds. Just raw execution streamed live to the world.

The Experiment

The Invisible Hand

The audience transforms from passive viewers into active participants. Watch the journey unfold in real-time. Vote on decisions. Shape who survives.

The show continues until someone wins. No scripts. No safety nets. Pure entrepreneurial chaos.

The Stream

Education at Speed

This is education at the speed of entertainment. Every builder's journey — the wins, the breakdowns, the 3am pivots — becomes a live case study for the next generation.

We're not predicting the future of work. We're streaming it.
